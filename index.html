About us

Local repair with a sustainability mission.

RenewTech Repair was founded in 2018 by electronics technician Lena Ortiz and environmental educator Marcus Chen. The company began as a small pop-up repair table at community events, fixing cracked phone screens and replacing laptop batteries. Today, RenewTech operates from Durham, New Hampshire, serving families, students, and small businesses while supporting the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals through responsible consumption, reuse, repair, and electronics recycling.

Why use us

Experienced technicians, transparent pricing, and less waste.

Clients choose RenewTech because we diagnose before we replace, use reclaimed or responsibly sourced parts when possible, provide clear repair reports, and offer local pickup options. Our convenient Durham location, certified technicians, one-year eligible repair warranty, and secure data-handling practices distinguish us from ordinary repair shops.

Policies

Clear policies before any repair begins.

Our store policies are written to make repair decisions simple, affordable, and fair.

Financing

Repairs under $100 are due at pickup. Repairs over $100 may be split into a 50% deposit and 50% final payment. For repairs over $300, customers may request a three-payment plan with approval before work begins. Diagnostic fees are waived when the repair is approved.

Delivery

Customers may pick up devices in store or request local delivery after repair completion. Local delivery is available within 10 miles of Durham. Bike courier delivery is used when available to reduce emissions. Devices are packaged in recyclable or reused protective materials.

Returns and warranty

Unopened accessories may be returned within 14 days with a receipt. Completed repairs are not refundable once approved and performed, but eligible parts and labor are covered by a one-year limited warranty. Damage caused by drops, liquid exposure, or unauthorized repairs is not covered.
